## What already does parts of this job

I searched GitHub first. [Beancount](https://github.com/beancount/beancount) already
does durable double-entry accounting. [Agenti](https://github.com/nirholas/agenti),
[Tychi Agent Finance](https://github.com/TychiWallet/agent-finance), and
[Fuin](https://github.com/Fuin-Labs/Fuin) already provide parts of agent wallets,
payments, and bounded authority. Those are useful layers to adopt or interoperate
with. Their primary project descriptions do not provide the missing disclosure
layer: a cross-venture record that keeps an observed transfer separate from a claim
that the transfer was revenue, with evidence attached field by field. That remains
the Census's job.
